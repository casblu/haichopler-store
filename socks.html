Paj ntaub, head to toe

Paj ntaub (“flower cloth”) is the Hmong textile art of embroidery and reverse appliqué, built from diamonds, crosses, spirals and sawtooth borders. Our socks borrow those bands and rows.

The Spirit Gold and Paper Boat designs are drawn from joss paper, which is used in offerings and funerals. We’re still deciding with our community how to release them.
